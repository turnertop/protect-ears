PROTECT EARS — EXPERIMENTAL macOS VOLUME GUARD

INSTALL
Requires macOS 13 or later. The app supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
No developer tools are needed for this download.

1. Drag Protect Ears.app into Applications.
2. If you opened a disk image, eject it after copying.
3. Open Protect Ears from Applications and click the ear icon in the menu bar.
4. The default ceiling is 4 of 16 volume steps (25%). You can select 5 steps.
5. Optionally enable Start at login after moving the app to Applications.

This release is ad-hoc signed and is NOT signed with an Apple Developer ID or
notarized by Apple. macOS may block it on first open. Only if you trust this
download, follow Apple's instructions for allowing this individual app:
https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

Do not disable Gatekeeper or remove quarantine attributes to install this app.

DISCLAIMER
We do not know whether Protect Ears will protect your hearing. It has not been
proven to prevent hearing damage. Do not rely on it as hearing protection.
The app can fail or react too late, and loud audio can still reach headphones.
Volume steps and percentages are not measured sound-pressure or safety limits.
