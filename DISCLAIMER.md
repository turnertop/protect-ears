# Hearing-safety disclaimer

**We do not know whether Protect Ears will protect your hearing. It has not been proven to prevent hearing damage. Do not rely on it as hearing protection.**

Protect Ears is experimental software that attempts to lower a macOS output-volume setting after it detects a change. It can fail, miss a change, or react too late. Loud audio, including brief spikes, can still reach your headphones before correction. Device connections, audio-profile changes, other apps, driver problems, system delays, and the app being stopped can interrupt its behavior.

The selected percentage and volume steps do not measure sound pressure at your ears and do not establish a safe listening level. A running indicator or successful volume check does not mean that your hearing is protected.

Protect Ears is not a medical device or a certified hearing-protection device. Its effectiveness in preventing hearing injury has not been clinically tested or validated.
